Welcome to Love n' Learn Childcare

Where Children Grow Into Confident, Independent Learners with Strong Self-Worth

At Love n' Learn Childcare Inc., our purpose is all about children and growing young learners. Creating an extension of the home atmosphere—while providing an environment that promotes professional care and early childhood education to the individual child—is what we are all about.

Our centre features bright, spacious classrooms for Infants, Toddlers, and Preschoolers along with a secure on-site outdoor playground. Our daily programs allow children to explore, investigate, and discover the world around them both indoors and outdoors while preparing for kindergarten and life beyond.

Classroom & Facility Overview

Inside Our Bright Classrooms & On-Site Playground

👶 Max 10 Infants

Infant Classroom (1–18 Mos)

Soft non-toxic flooring, separate serene sleep area with solid wood cribs, sensory mirrors, and cozy feeding nook.

🧸 Max 15 Toddlers

Toddler Classroom (18–30 Mos)

Low open-ended wooden shelves, dramatic play kitchen, reading corner, sensory tables, and child-sized washrooms.

🎨 Jr & Sr Cohorts

Preschool Classrooms (2.5–4.5 Yrs)

Dedicated Junior & Senior Preschool rooms equipped for early literacy, math manipulatives, science inquiry, and Kindergarten readiness.

🛝 On-Site Facility

Fenced Outdoor Playground & Kitchen

Secure, fenced on-site outdoor playground for daily active play, plus our commercial nut-free kitchen where hot lunches are cooked fresh.

Procare Child Care Mobile App

Love n' Learn Childcare uses the Procare Child Care Mobile App for all parent communication, daily activity logs, contactless QR/GPS check-in, and touchless tuition billing.

How Enrolled Parents Sign In:
1. Download the free Procare: Child Care App on iOS (4.9★) or Android (4.8★).
2. Use the invitation link sent to your email by our Director at info@lovenlearn.ca.
3. Need help resetting your PIN or QR code? Call us at (905) 201-4000.
